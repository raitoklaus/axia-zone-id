This sample post demonstrates how blog articles, upcoming event schedules, and the local performer wiki stay tightly connected using Obsidian-style `[[Wiki Links]]`.

## Inline Wiki Links

- Prefixed performer link: [[performers/hana-minase]]
- Bare-slug performer link: [[ren-takahashi]]
- Aliased event link: [[events/axia-winter-live-2026|Axia Winter Live 2026]]
- Past archive reference: [[events/jakarta-anifest-summer-2026]]

## Standalone Wiki Link Cards

Place a `[[Wiki Link]]` alone on its own paragraph to render a rich summary card:

[[events/axia-winter-live-2026]]

[[performers/hana-minase]]

> [!NOTE]
> Performer and event cards automatically pull metadata from `src/content/performers/` and `src/content/events/`, while `lineup` references populate each performer's Upcoming and Past Appearances.
