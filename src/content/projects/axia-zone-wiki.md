**Axia Zone ID** combines an editorial anime/otaku blog with a structured Indonesian ACG event schedule and local performer wiki.

- Browse upcoming and archived events in List View or Monthly Calendar View.
- Track performer appearances automatically via structured `lineup` references.
- Publish from desktop or mobile using Pages CMS or Sveltia CMS.
